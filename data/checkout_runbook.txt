Checkout Incident Runbook

For a failed PayPal Pay Later checkout, first capture a unique correlation ID and
checkout attempt timestamp. Verify API gateway and payment-orchestration logs for
the same correlation ID. Check whether the provider returned a timeout, declined
payment, or completed authorization. Never retry a possibly successful authorization
without checking idempotency keys and the existing payment state.

Severity-1 checkout incidents require on-call escalation immediately. Gather logs,
service dashboards, the affected region, and customer impact estimates. Escalate
to the incident commander, then execute the approved mitigation runbook.
